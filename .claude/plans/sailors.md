## Navy & Marine Inspired Names
Ensign Ace – A young officer who serves as the team's top scorer.
Riptide Rookie – A fresh recruit with high energy and fast spikes.
Captain Cove – The strategic leader who controls the net.
Marine Spike – A tough, defensive sailor with powerful serves.
Sailor Smash – The heavy hitter who hits the ball with force.
Admiral Anchor – The tanky character who defends the backline.
Pilot Net – A fast, agile sailor who jumps high for blocks.
Deckhand Dunk – A character who uses the deck of a ship as a launching pad.

## Ocean & Volleyball Fusion
Surge Serve – A sailor whose attack mimics a crashing wave.
Tide Turner – The support character who can reverse the flow of the match.
Foam Flier – A speedy character who "flies" over the net.
Coral Claw – A defensive specialist with sharp, grabbing attacks.
Deep Blue – A mysterious, powerful sailor with a "deep water" ultimate attack.
Wave Walker – A sailor who moves effortlessly across the sandy court.
Seagull Set – A support character who sets up perfect passes with a dive.
Reef Rager – A chaotic sailor who thrives in messy, close-quarters plays.

## Fun & Quirky (Casual Vibe)
Salty Serve – A veteran sailor with a "salty" attitude and tricky shots.
Net Nerf – A character who specializes in tricky, unexpected blocks.
Anchor Drop – A sailor who smashes the ball down like a dropped anchor.
Driftwood Dunk – A relaxed, casual sailor who hits surprisingly hard.
Stormy Spike – A character whose attacks get stronger as the "weather" changes.
Shell Shock – A sailor who uses seashells as projectiles or defensive gear.
